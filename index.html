The Story So Far

You began as prisoners on a stolen ship. Fifteen sessions later, you have freed an enslaved giant, humiliated the Agile Fleet, rescued Flint, burned half of Dolphinia by accident and/or choice, entered Wendel Wood, discovered that Axiom is Ash Blackwell, broken an infernal extraction site, made Axiom Mayor of a ruined town, watched Ember become a Stars Keeper, found three separate dragon mysteries, lost Kane, and acquired a criminal lead pointing toward Machi Town. The sea is significantly less simple than it looked in Session 1.

Session Recaps 1–15

Historical Rule

01Session 1 - Escape from the Tempest's MercyThe campaign begins in chains and ends with the party owning the ship that imprisoned them.⌄

What happened

  • Kaelis, Ember, Jun Taka, GUTR, Isla Drake, and Axiom-7 begin imprisoned aboard the Tempest's Mercy on the Everpass Sea.
  • Ember uses telepathy and deception to help start the escape; the bluff turns into a fight with Agile Fleet guards.
  • Jarlin, another prisoner, claims the Agile Fleet killed his crew and stole his map.
  • In the captain's quarters, Jarlin is found over the dead Caederick Deadwater. He recovers a map; when handed over, it evaporates, and Jarlin escapes by dinghy.
  • Axiom opens a bolted bow plate and the crew discovers a massive sea giant/troll chained beneath the ship and being used as a living engine.
  • The crew frees the creature, stabilizes the ship, claims the Tempest's Mercy, and reaches Dolphinia.

What the crew learned

  • The Agile Fleet uses enslaved sea giants/trolls to power ships.
  • Caederick Deadwater is dead; Jarlin is a dangerous map-connected mystery.
  • Dolphinia becomes the crew's first major port.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Caederick's engraved compass; Kaelis' +1 Charisma skull necklace; captured ship papers, weapons, rations, and the Tempest's Mercy itself.
02Session 2 - Isla's Map and the Flint SetupThe crew inventories its new ship, learns more about Isla's Deadwater history, and discovers Flint Blackwell is marked for execution.⌄

What happened

  • The ship is inventoried: swivel guns, firearms, melee weapons, water, rum, ale, medical and repair supplies.
  • Isla reveals she served as Darius Deadwater's first mate for about twenty years and was passed over when command went to Caederick.
  • Isla reveals a secret map stitched into her clothing.
  • Kaelis consults Thalryx and learns the distant/hard-to-pinpoint route is tied to Rhysand.
  • Bonjovi warns of Flint Blackwell's coming execution.
  • The crew gathers intelligence in Finnegan's Tavern and buys haste/invisibility potions from Alyse.
  • Axiom and GUTR steal pigs Henry and Bao.
  • The Frying Dutch Pan and his strange handmade compass enter ship life.

What the crew learned

  • The Frying Dutch Pan's compass is eventually understood as pointing toward desire, roots, or personal truth rather than north.
  • Flint Blackwell matters enough to the Agile Fleet to warrant a guarded execution.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Three Potions of Haste, two Potions of Invisibility, Henry and Bao, and the ship inventory.
03Session 3 - Bart, Forged Orders, and the Safe HeistThe party turns from surviving the Agile Fleet to actively manipulating it.⌄

What happened

  • After disposing of bodies near the docks, the crew nearly gets caught by Bonjovi and hides the evidence underwater.
  • Kaelis nearly drowns and Jun resuscitates him.
  • The crew discovers a Bag of Beans.
  • GUTR befriends Bart the crow and discovers he can communicate with him.
  • The party learns Axleton Bulkin is a major Agile Fleet power.
  • Kaelis and Ember forge Agile Fleet orders to rotate Flint's guards; the crew makes a fake seal from carved wood and wax.
  • Kaelis and Axiom scout the execution site.
  • The party steals Finnegan's roughly 300-pound safe and escapes with 350 gp.
  • Bart delivers the forged orders before dawn.

What the crew learned

  • Forged authority and disguise are now proven pirate tools for the crew.
  • Bart becomes a recurring messenger/scout.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Bag of Beans; 350 gp; forged Agile Fleet orders and seal.
04Session 4 - Flint's Rescue and the Tavern InfernoThe forged-order plan works until it spectacularly does not.⌄

What happened

  • The Tempest crew impersonates Agile Fleet relief personnel to reach Flint.
  • Flint polymorphs into a crow and hides in Jun's hat.
  • Whiskey, fog, darkness, and open flame turn the tavern into a battlefield.
  • Kaelis fights inside Darkness with Devil's Sight; Axiom uses firearms and paladin power; Jun and Isla execute precise melee attacks.
  • Axiom finds Finnegan's journal and a sketch of Heirennesy.
  • Axiom breaks an escape route and Flint escapes in crow form.
  • Bart realizes his message helped save Flint.
  • The party reaches Level 5.

What the crew learned

  • Flint is free and becomes a major Blackwell information source.
  • The crew is no longer merely hiding from the Agile Fleet; it is openly disrupting Fleet control.

Notable loot / state changes

  • 30 gp, six shotgun shells, Finnegan's journal, Heirennesy sketch.
05Session 5 - Jeoffrey Returns and the Little Empire BattleMercy from Session 1 pays off when the freed giant returns as an ally during an Agile Fleet attack.⌄

What happened

  • Farmer John joins the group with five pigs, including Giuseppe.
  • Jeoffrey, the sea giant/troll freed from the Tempest's Mercy, returns.
  • Jeoffrey is connected to Thalryx and the returning giant problem.
  • Agile Fleet forces attack near the Little Empire and civilians flee.
  • The party and Jeoffrey fight through the Fleet line; Farmer John provides ranged support.
  • The final Fleet officer is defeated and the remaining ships retreat.
  • Dolphinia civilians witness both the Fleet defeat and the truth that giants had been enslaved.

What the crew learned

  • Freeing Jeoffrey had lasting consequences; not every giant is the enemy.
  • The Agile Fleet's use of enslaved giants becomes public knowledge on Dolphinia.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Battlefield loot is gathered at the start of Session 6.
06Session 6 - Garrick, Dashule's Blade, and the Elias TreatyA victory-lap loot session turns into arson, death, major magic items, and a political meeting with Elias Crowe.⌄

What happened

  • The party divides major battlefield finds: Berserker Axe, Immovable Rod, Rope of Entanglement, Javelin of Lightning, coin, and GUTR's silver earring.
  • Garrick tells the legend of Dashule Lepravonci and the gold-brimmed hat that completes the legendary katana.
  • Jun is tested, judged worthy, and claims the incomplete katana. His uncle Hongbin may know more in Wendel Wood.
  • Isla purchases Shadow Fang.
  • The Garrick confrontation escalates; Ember's Fireball helps destroy the shop and the party kills Garrick.
  • Farmer John and all five of his pigs die in the burning-shop aftermath.
  • Groknar and Borin Stonehand investigate; the party persuades them it was defending Dolphinia from Agile threats.
  • The party is taken to the Mountain Castle and meets Headmaster Vailrithe Bulregard, Elias Crowe, and Gideon.
  • Vailrithe signs a protection treaty with Elias.
  • Kaelis confirms his pact priorities center on his missing son; Axiom reveals more of his history as an Elias-created weapon/tool.

What the crew learned

  • Elias Crowe is a major power separate from the Agile Fleet.
  • Vailrithe sees giant slavery as unacceptable but still chooses Elias' protection for Dolphinia.
  • Axiom's loyalty is to the crew, not to a god or king.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Dashule katana to Jun; Shadow Fang to Isla; major magic-item haul from the Fleet battle.
07Session 7 - The Tempest Vanguard Is NamedThe crew leaves Dolphinia with new goblin shipmates, more Blackwell history, and a permanent crew name.⌄

What happened

  • Gideon's comments create another crack in Axiom's missing childhood memories.
  • Axiom states clearly that his paladin oath/power is tied to loyalty to the crew.
  • The party searches Farmer John's abandoned home and recovers pig-related belongings and supplies.
  • Fred and Shmelly are recruited as permanent ship crew.
