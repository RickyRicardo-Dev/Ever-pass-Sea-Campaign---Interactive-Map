Session 17 — Shrimp, Old Homes, and the Wave at Filiorum

  • Bob's permanent new name is Nugget.
  • In Machi Town, the crew impersonated local guards, got tangled in Jose and Luis's shrimp-boat dispute with Soulskins Steel Mill, and pressured the mill into paying Jose 800 gp.
  • Luis went overboard during the shrimp trip and was never recovered; the table treated him as likely dead. Jose surrendered his belongings under pressure, and the crew opened a locked chest containing his mother's unusual +1 medium armor.
  • A contact was hired for 200 gp to hunt Bart, with 100 gp paid up front and the rest due after proof of the kill.
  • At Hallows Reach, Isla collected Jeremy's finished fire-resistant curtains, cloak, and Tempest's Mercy flag. Ember returned to her childhood home and learned that the magic book she remembered was written in Nancy Blackwell's hand and effectively rewrote magic.
  • Brathe asked to join the Tempest's Mercy as a blacksmith. Isla considered a trial basis if Axiom approved, but Brathe boarded without permission and was ordered back off the ship, so his place aboard is still unresolved.
  • Good Morrow Juice was revealed to use a valuable root from Filiorum Silva. A hooded informant also pointed the crew toward Arnold Bernard on Prisonia Isle, saying the man who collected Flint's old bounty now has about 100,000 silver pieces left.
  • The Tempest's Mercy is fully repaired; its cannons and dry fuses were maintained by Flint. Food is now at 11 days of rations.
  • The session ended at Filiorum Silva after reports of an earthquake. A roughly 60-foot tsunami surged over 40-foot coastal rockwork toward the settlement, and Kaelis experienced a vision of Marica guiding the wave.

Campaign spine

The Story So Far

The Tempest Vanguard began as prisoners aboard an Agile Fleet ship and escaped with the Tempest's Mercy. On Dolphinia they learned the Fleet's power rests on cruelty and secrets, rescued Flint Blackwell from execution, and helped expose enslaved sea beings. Their voyage pushed them toward Wendel Wood, where Ember's Blackwell history became inseparable from dying Great Trees, dragons, old Keeper oaths, and the scars left by Elias Crowe and his allies.

In Hallows Reach and Lorell, Ember became a formal Stars Keeper while Axiom learned he was Ash Blackwell, was chosen by Lorell's Tree, became Mayor, and eventually recovered the blue crystal tied to his childhood transformation. Isla's Deadwater past also returned: Kane disappeared, Bart revealed his vampire identity, and Darius Deadwater admitted he had been watching Isla through old crew members.

Session 17 pulled the campaign toward Filiorum Silva. After a bizarre Machi Town shrimp-boat detour, Ember revisited her childhood home and learned more about Nancy's magic. The crew learned Arnold Bernard is on Prisonia, discovered Good Morrow Juice uses Filiorum Root, and then sailed east after news of an earthquake. The session ended with a massive tsunami approaching Filiorum while Kaelis saw Marica guiding the wave.

CAMPFIRE THEORY — NOT CONFIRMED CANON

The Root That Remembers

Good Morrow Juice uses a rare Filiorum Root, and everyone drinking it starts acting strangely “fancy.” What if the root isn't just magical flavoring? What if roots cut from the Filii Tree region hold tiny emotional impressions from the land—and drinking them makes people echo whatever the Tree is feeling? If that theory is even half right, the earthquake and tsunami could make the next batch of Good Morrow Juice very different from the last one.

This is deliberately a fun player-facing mystery spun from loose lore. It is not confirmed truth unless play makes it true.
