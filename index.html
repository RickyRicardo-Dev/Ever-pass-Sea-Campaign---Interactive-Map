Session Recaps 1–16

What happened at the table, what the crew learned, and what changed. Open any session for details.

Historical Rule
These recaps preserve what the party knew at the time. Later sessions may reveal an old assumption was incomplete, but the original event remains part of the story.
01Session 1 - Escape from the Tempest's MercyThe campaign begins in chains and ends with the party owning the ship that imprisoned them.⌄

What happened

  • Kaelis, Ember, Jun Taka, GUTR, Isla Drake, and Axiom-7 begin imprisoned aboard the Tempest's Mercy on the Everpass Sea.
  • Ember uses telepathy and deception to help start the escape; the bluff turns into a fight with Agile Fleet guards.
  • Jarlin, another prisoner, claims the Agile Fleet killed his crew and stole his map.
  • In the captain's quarters, Jarlin is found over the dead Caederick Deadwater. He recovers a map; when handed over, it evaporates, and Jarlin escapes by dinghy.
  • Axiom opens a bolted bow plate and the crew discovers a massive sea giant/troll chained beneath the ship and being used as a living engine.
  • The crew frees the creature, stabilizes the ship, claims the Tempest's Mercy, and reaches Dolphinia.

What the crew learned

  • The Agile Fleet uses enslaved sea giants/trolls to power ships.
  • Caederick Deadwater is dead; Jarlin is a dangerous map-connected mystery.
  • Dolphinia becomes the crew's first major port.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Caederick's engraved compass; Kaelis' +1 Charisma skull necklace; captured ship papers, weapons, rations, and the Tempest's Mercy itself.
02Session 2 - Isla's Map and the Flint SetupThe crew inventories its new ship, learns more about Isla's Deadwater history, and discovers Flint Blackwell is marked for execution.⌄

What happened

  • The ship is inventoried: swivel guns, firearms, melee weapons, water, rum, ale, medical and repair supplies.
  • Isla reveals she served as Darius Deadwater's first mate for about twenty years and was passed over when command went to Caederick.
  • Isla reveals a secret map stitched into her clothing.
  • Kaelis consults Thalryx and learns the distant/hard-to-pinpoint route is tied to Rhysand.
  • Bonjovi warns of Flint Blackwell's coming execution.
  • The crew gathers intelligence in Finnegan's Tavern and buys haste/invisibility potions from Alyse.
  • Axiom and GUTR steal pigs Henry and Bao.
  • The Frying Dutch Pan and his strange handmade compass enter ship life.

What the crew learned

  • The Frying Dutch Pan's compass is eventually understood as pointing toward desire, roots, or personal truth rather than north.
  • Flint Blackwell matters enough to the Agile Fleet to warrant a guarded execution.

Notable loot / state changes

  • Three Potions of Haste, two Potions of Invisibility, Henry and Bao, and the ship inventory.

09Session 9 - Green Water, the Dragon Sword, and Wendel WoodThe crew discovers an ancient water system, survives the gold chamber, and finally sees Wendel Wood.⌄

What happened

  • The crew fights a goblin boarding party while the ship is grounded in the ravine.
  • Axiom shoots a pulsing green orb whose light returns to a central hub.
  • Three characters experience a shared underwater vision; Isla is definitely one of them. The scene includes underwater war, a crowned giant/sea figure, and a woman in white associated with Marica.

  • The crew raids a goblin village/ridge.

  • Flint loses flesh from one finger to the liquid.
  • After roughly 100 gp or more is offered, the chamber presents the Dragon Sword.
  • Kaelis keeps custody of the sword, but it requires 19 Strength to wield normally and gains +3d6 damage against dragon-type creatures.
  • Flint tells Ember the brother she believed she killed actually survived, was gravely injured, and was taken away under her father's direction.
  • The crew finally sees Wendel Wood: four colossal trees, waterways, a huge plateau/mountain, and a prominent monastery.

What the crew learned

  • Ember's brother survived the childhood incident.
  • The Dragon Sword strongly foreshadows a coming dragon threat.
  • Wendel Wood's Great Trees and monastery are real and enormous.

Notable loot / state changes

  • 12 gp, Wendel Wood map, fake golden mustache, bombs/net launcher, green condensed-water material, Dragon Sword.

16Session 16 - Masks Off in Machi TownBart's disguise falls, Lorell gets a school plan, Machi Town exposes the Mob's money network, and Axiom finally recovers the crystal that began his childhood mystery.⌄

From Lorell's ruins to a real school

  • The session resumes after the three Lorell Mob ambushers from Session 15 are dead. Hallows Reach locals show little sympathy for the Mob.
  • Axiom and the crew push Lorell's rebuild beyond patching houses: they want a proper classroom, books, a teacher, working farms, and a legitimate economy.
  • Argyle — the familiar goblin shipwright/repair lead — is shown plans for a classroom roughly 27 feet long and 23 feet wide, with about 25 desks, bookshelves, and a teacher area.
  • Argyle initially quotes around 600 gp for a quality build; the crew negotiates the project to 500 gp and asks that it be started as soon as possible.
  • Lorell residents explain that before the devastation the Mob pushed much of the town's money. With the Mob gone, the settlement has almost no economy, damaged farms, only a few usable houses, and relies heavily on cheap bread brought from Hallows Reach.
  • The Vanguard instructs residents to reclaim farmable ground, help Argyle, and start building useful work and food production around the school project.

Ember's Keeper bond pushes back

  • Ember's Level 1 Stars Keeper bond begins producing a physical warning: when her anger crosses the line, she feels a painful internal twinge.
  • The crew starts treating that reaction as immediate feedback that Ember's Keeper path is changing how her choices affect her.

Bob joins the chaos

  • Ember befriends a raccoon in the forest and names him Bob. Comprehend Languages allows the party to actually converse with him.
  • Later in Machi Town, Luinda's Boutique sells a transformation necklace for 201 gp after a successful negotiation. Bob is transformed into a cat, and the necklace cannot simply be taken off.

Bart is not just a bird

  • Suspicion around Bart finally boils over. Dispel Magic strips away his form and reveals a human man underneath.
  • He identifies himself as Bart Roberts / Bartholomew Roberts / Black Bart Roberts.
  • Bart says he is a vampire whose transformation form is a crow rather than a bat.
  • Most importantly, Bart says Darius Deadwater sent both him and Kane to quietly watch/protect Isla and report back. He says he told Darius that Isla was okay and that the Tempest's Mercy was in her name.
  • Kane is therefore no longer just a missing crewmate: according to Bart, he was the second Darius operative. His whereabouts remain unknown, and Bart's earlier “Blue Lizard” clue is still unresolved.

Bart's account of Darius and the vampire crew

  • Bart says that after Caederick sided with the Agile Fleet, Darius rallied members of his old crew, built another ship, and went after his son.
  • Bart claims that crew boarded the Tempest's Mercy and killed Caederick. This creates an unresolved question because Session 1 also places Jarlin in the captain's quarters over Caederick's body.
  • According to Bart, the rebuilt crew was later attacked by a huge flying craft described as a dragon skull / skull of a dragon. It passed overhead and dropped three large bombs, sinking their ship.
  • Bart says the next thing he knew, the crew awoke alive and Darius had made a deal: they had become vampires. The attacker, the party Darius bargained with, and the price of the deal are not established.
  • Isla sends Bart back with an ultimatum for Darius: she thought he was dead, wants him to stop infiltrating her crew and playing with her head, and wants him to act dead rather than keep interfering.

Arrival in Machi Town

  • The party heads from Lorell toward Machi Town and avoids another long, swaying bridge by using magical flight.
  • Machi Town is nothing like ruined Lorell: it is lush, lively, prosperous, full of families, cherry blossoms, a water wheel, and carefully built Japanese-style wooden architecture.
  • The town is also heavily secured. Guards are stationed around shops and businesses because of Lorell Mob activity.
  • Ken Fujimoto, a town guard, confirms the Mob has been a real enough problem to justify that protection.

The Mob's money trail

  • The crew questions local businesses and eventually reaches Zen Bozita at the steel mill.
  • Zen admits he runs money through the Lorell Mob and that the mill/business acts as a front to keep the funds looking legitimate — direct confirmation of money laundering.
  • Zen says the Mob operates a very large ship at the docks and points the crew toward Giuseppe, a front-dock contact.
  • The exact top leader of the Lorell Mob is still not clearly established by the session.

Shops and new contacts

  • Sally's Potions is still operating even though Sally is said to have died roughly three years earlier; Susan works the shop now.
  • At Luinda's Boutique, Luinda Helsing reveals that vampire hunting runs in her family. The crew gives her Bart as a lead, and she agrees to investigate the vampire network/hideout and provide more information later.
  • Luinda says the animal-speech magic Ember wants would require the heart of a dying dragon. A substitute/small fake heart attempt fails.
  • Frankie, a halfling cigar merchant, and several other shopkeepers are encountered while the crew searches for Mob information.

Boarding the Lorell Mob ship

  • At the docks, a confrontation with a Mob-aligned guard ends with the guard dead and his body kicked into the water.
  • The party uses disguises and a loose prisoner/new-recruit cover to board the Mob's large ship.
  • They work deeper into the ship and encounter Turresque Crowe, a heavily mechanical figure protected by guards.
  • The confrontation erupts into a close-quarters battle. Turresque fights with a glowing red sword while the Vanguard attacks his armored mechanical body.

Axiom's crystal comes home

  • During the fight, a blue light shines from inside Turresque. Axiom tears open his metal chest and finds the long-missing blue crystal from his childhood goggles embedded inside him.
  • Turresque dies. The Lorell Mob crystal trail — Brathe → Finch → Mob → Machi Town — is physically resolved.
  • The moment Axiom removes the crystal, blue energy flashes and the surrounding world stops. Other people and objects are frozen in place while the Vanguard can still move.
  • Axiom places the crystal back into his childhood goggles. Through them he sees a large square/oblong shape during the stopped-world event. The meaning of that shape and the source of the time effect are unknown.

Loot and end state

  • Turresque's body yields a glowing red longsword and the party initially finds 150 gp.
  • Rather than spend the rest of the night searching room by room, the DM fast-resolves the ship's remaining loot: each participating character receives an additional 100 gp.
  • Each participating character also earns one magic item of rare rarity or lower, with the player choosing an option and sending it to the DM for approval.
  • The Vanguard leaves the Mob ship and returns to a nearby tree to rest.
  • The Bonsai Tree still needs a Keeper, the monastery is still unresolved, Kane is still missing, Luinda is investigating the vampire lead, and the Lorell Mob remains bigger than Turresque alone.
