Axiom-7

Ash Blackwell • Warforged Paladin • Mayor of Lorell
ACTIVETREE-CHOSENNOT YET FORMAL KEEPERCRYSTAL RECOVERED — S16
IdentityAxiom-7, the mechanical survivor of the human life once called Ash Blackwell.
FamilySon of Brathe and Nancy Blackwell; Ember Blackwell is his sister.
CallingPaladin whose loyalty centers on freedom, courage, and the crew rather than a king or institution.
Current officeMayor of Lorell.
Great TreeAsh Tree / Tree of Scientia Regnant / Tree of Lorellious.
Current stateChosen by the Tree and carrying its living green leaf, but still not formally attuned as a Keeper through Session 16.
Known Backstory

Ash grew up around Gearford with Gideon, fascinated by sailors, machinery, and the wider sea. Gideon left to sail while Ash stayed behind for a time, working around the harbor and listening to sailors’ stories.

Gideon later returned with a functional mechanical arm and eventually brought Ash aboard the Mourning Tide under Elias Crowe. Ash woke restrained on an ancient metal table while machinery forced metal into his body. When consciousness returned, the system identified him as AXIOM-7.

Elias used Axiom as a weapon for boardings, suppression, and dangerous operations. The defining break came when Axiom began choosing for himself. His later loyalty to the Tempest Vanguard is important precisely because it is chosen rather than programmed.

Axiom does not need to “become Ash again.” Current canon treats Ash as his human history and Axiom-7 as the identity of the person who survived.

Personality & Character Core
  • Extremely literal and mechanically observant, with a long-running habit of counting ship parts, wood, sticks, and structural pieces.
  • Protective, loyal, and unusually serious about freedom and consent because his own body and identity were taken from him.
  • He often processes emotion through action: protecting someone, fixing something, building something, or standing his ground rather than talking at length about how he feels.
  • His strongest theme is autonomy: who gets to decide what a person is, what they owe, and whether protection becomes another form of ownership.

Known Backstory

Jun comes from a disciplined island sword tradition and was trained from childhood in the Way of the Samurai. The original supplied backstory described him as one of his homeland’s most gifted young swordsmen.

His desire for adventure was awakened by travelers and stories from outside his home. He left not because he had failed there, but because mastery inside one world was no longer enough.

The campaign’s newest canon deliberately leaves the formal name and broader politics of Jun’s home island unresolved. The important surviving truth is the culture: discipline, swordsmanship, ancestry, ritual, and the expectation of greatness.

Personality & Character Core
  • Composed, proud, disciplined, and capable of making extreme violence look ceremonial.
  • Jun is not simply the “serious samurai” of the crew. Actual play repeatedly shows that he enjoys the crew’s absurdity and can be affectionate, playful, and theatrical.
  • His central tension is greatness versus loyalty. The sword legacy does not merely ask whether he can win—it increasingly asks what kind of person deserves the power he wants.

Known Backstory

GUTR was found as a horned red-skinned infant in a woven basket and burlap sack. The exact port and circumstances of his abandonment have never been cleanly established.

Pirates raised him collectively. Because his size and strength were useful, he was trained as a soldier rather than educated in the way most children would be.

Rage sometimes overtook him even though he understood himself as good. By adulthood his violence had created a frightening reputation, but the people closest to him knew a completely different man: quiet, giving, protective, and willing to offer food, clothes, labor, or physical comfort without expecting anything back.

He often watches the sea from the bow. The sea is not simply where he works—it is the closest thing he has to a homeland.

Personality & Character Core
  • Acts of service are his clearest love language.
  • Physically expressive: his tails, posture, purr-like contented sounds, touch, and protective positioning often communicate more than his Common vocabulary can.
  • He can be extremely funny, but his speech should not erase his interior life. The core character is a man everyone expected to be a weapon learning that he can also be loved as a person.
  • His rage is most interesting when it conflicts with the gentle person he wants to be.

Isla Drake

Captain • Drow Rogue / Navigator • Tempest’s Mercy
ACTIVECAPTAINDEADWATER VETERANBART/DARIUS THREAD ACTIVE
AppearanceLight-purple drow sailor with long silver hair.

Old lifeFormer first mate under Darius Deadwater for roughly twenty years.
PhilosophyLoyalty is rare, valuable currency—an idea the campaign keeps challenging as the crew becomes chosen family.
Known Backstory

Isla was born in a busy port city where she watched ordinary workers lose their lives to fees, contracts, and people with more power. She learned early that competence did not guarantee respect or safety.

As a teenager on a merchant vessel she learned navigation, negotiation, and practical violence. After pirates boarded one voyage and the survivors were cheated of promised pay, she concluded that the pirates had not necessarily worked harder or acted better—they had simply taken what they wanted and nobody stopped them.

On a later voyage she quietly made another pirate boarding easier and then sold her usefulness to the victors: routes, cargo habits, weak captains, and operational knowledge. She rose through piracy until she became Darius Deadwater’s First Mate aboard the Tempest’s Mercy.

During a later coup/mutiny she fed information and advice to both sides while pursuing the thing she actually wanted: an old map. She stole it and left. That combination—survival, betrayal, competence, and self-authorship—still shapes how she leads.

Personality & Character Core
  • Pragmatic, observant, strategically social, and constantly aware of exits, leverage, routes, and danger.
  • Her transactional instincts are a survival mechanism. “Loyalty is currency” keeps her from becoming the person who is surprised by betrayal.
  • Captaincy is changing the meaning of that philosophy. The Tempest Vanguard increasingly follows her because they choose to, and she now has people whose dreams, lives, and chaos reflect on her command.
  • She does not need to become soft to become a better captain. Her strongest growth is responsibility without losing the sharp instincts that kept her alive.

Kaelis

Tiefling Warlock • Pirate Father • Pact-Bound to Thalryx

Current gear/story assetsDragon Sword in custody, Heraldr magical cat companion, +1 Charisma skull necklace; old limited fire-resistance cloak destroyed.
Known Backstory

Kaelis grew up in the gutters of a pirate port and learned theft and smuggling young. Fatherhood was not originally part of his plan, but after Tina died during fever season, Jeff became the center of his life.

One final theft was supposed to secure money: a barnacle-crusted chest holding a black coral idol shaped like a crowned figure in chains. A storm swallowed the docks; the idol and Jeff disappeared, and a voice told Kaelis that Jeff was not lost—he was claimed.

Kaelis entered a pact with Thalryx. His power is therefore not simple worship; it is tied to a parent negotiating with the being that holds leverage over his child.

That makes Kaelis one of the campaign’s sharpest contradictions: he fights slavery and helps free others while living under a pact that can threaten the person he loves most.

Personality & Character Core
  • Protective, pragmatic, criminally creative, and willing to cross lines quickly when he thinks the goal matters.
  • Kaelis is deeply pirate-coded: stealing, taking ships, making trouble, exploiting openings, and finding the fastest route to money or leverage are not side jokes—they are part of what makes him feel like himself.
  • His most dangerous trait is not greed. It is love under coercion. He has openly admitted that if the choice becomes Jeff or the crew, he will choose Jeff.
  • His humor and chaos can hide how serious that statement actually is.

Known Backstory

Kane is unusual among the playable-character list because most of his backstory has been revealed at the table rather than supplied as a long pre-campaign biography.

He belonged to Darius Deadwater’s old crew and knew Isla during her years as First Mate. He later became disillusioned with Darius’s methods and says he eventually left.

Kane remembers dying and waking after a supernatural restoration. He described once having a bond to the being involved in bringing him back, then later severing that bond when he left.

Direct campaign canon also establishes that Kane carries secret orders from Darius, but the exact wording has never been cleanly revealed and should not be invented.

Personality & Character Core
  • Kane presents as experienced, secretive, capable, and much less shocked by supernatural politics than the newer crew.
  • He carries the weight of the Deadwater era into the present. His value is not just vampiric power—it is that he remembers people and decisions Isla’s current crew only knows as history.
  • His secrecy is currently the defining trust problem. He joined the crew while still carrying Darius’s purpose.

Known Backstory

Ember’s magic was not learned so much as survived. Anger sparked flame, fear broke glass, and distress distorted the air. A childhood fire taught the people around her to fear what she might become.

Isolation pushed her toward a hard survival strategy: if people already thought she was dangerous, power and intimidation felt safer than constantly begging to be seen as good.

Malakar Voss later taught Ember discipline and structure. Control became something she actively practiced rather than something she naturally possessed.

The central wound of her early life was Ash. Ember spent years believing she had killed her brother when her magic got out of control. Wendel Wood eventually revealed that the truth was more complicated: Ash had survived, the childhood experiments were consensual attempts to understand his Tree/crystal phenomenon, and Ember’s anger/frustration drove one experiment catastrophically too far.

Personality & Character Core
  • Externally observant and often controlled; internally intense, defensive, emotional, and capable of both deep protection and frightening cruelty.
  • She often wants to nurture animals and vulnerable creatures even while threatening people five minutes later. That contradiction is part of the character rather than an error.
  • Her moral identity is active work. Ember does not need to prove that she has no darkness; the question is whether fear, guilt, and anger get to make her decisions for her.
  • Keeperhood makes that personal struggle visible to the world: power is inherited, but duty and restraint are chosen.
