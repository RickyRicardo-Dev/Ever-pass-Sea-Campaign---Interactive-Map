Character Directory

Characters

One organized place for the crew, allies, NPCs, families, factions, and canonical name spellings. NPCs are grouped by the region or storyline where players are most likely to remember them.

Tempest Vanguard

Core crew
Captain • Drow sailor/rogue • Tempest Vanguard

Isla Drake

Captain of the Tempest's Mercy. Former first mate under Darius Deadwater for roughly twenty years. Wants her own name known across the seas and currently cares more about being a pirate captain than the Tree crisis.

Current thread

Darius, Bart and Kane have dragged her old crew back into her life. She told Darius to stop watching and manipulating her crew.

Tiefling warlock • Father • Pirate • Tempest Vanguard

Kaelis

Bound to Thalryx the Drowned Sovereign while searching for his missing son Jeff. He would do almost anything to find him.

Current thread

Session 17 ends with Kaelis seeing a vision of Marica guiding the tsunami toward Filiorum Silva—the region tied to his intended Great Tree.

Arcane caster • Level 1 Stars Keeper • Blackwell family / Tempest Vanguard

Ember Blackwell

Fire, grief, family history, and the Great Trees all collide in Ember. Her Keeper bond now gives physical feedback when her choices cross certain lines.

Current thread

Session 17 revealed that the childhood magic book carried Nancy Blackwell's handwriting and techniques that effectively rewrote magic.

Warforged paladin • Mayor of Lorell • Blackwell history / Tempest Vanguard

Axiom-7

Ash Blackwell is Axiom's human history; Axiom-7 is his current identity. He is Tree-chosen, Mayor of Lorell, and still not a formal Keeper through Session 17.

Current thread

Recovered the blue crystal in Session 16; its time-freezing behavior is confirmed, but its full purpose remains unknown.

Samurai warrior • Tempest Vanguard

Jun Taka

A disciplined sword heir whose greatness sometimes outruns his understanding of the legacy attached to him.

Current thread

Dashule Lepravonci and Jun's legendary blade remain major open personal threads.

Two-tailed tiefling • Pirate-raised warrior • Tempest Vanguard

GUTR

Huge, feared in appearance, but often gentle and generous. Limited Common never means limited thought.

Current thread

His twin tails, old ring, loneliness, and connection to the Bicaudis imagery remain unresolved.

Vampire rogue • Missing • Deadwater-era survivor

Kane Zarovic

Joined in Session 13, recognized Thalryx, and admitted carrying secret orders from Darius.

Current thread

Still missing at the end of Session 17.

Families

Blackwell Family

Ember, Axiom/Ash, Brathe, Nancy, Flint. Their family history is tied to Wendel Wood, old magic, the Great Trees, and the Keeper problem.

Bulkin Family

Powerful line tied to the Agile Fleet and the Blackwell split. Axelton Bulkin remains a major hostile legacy figure.

Deadwater Family & Crew

Darius, Caederick, Isla’s twenty-year service, Bart and Kane. The question is no longer whether the past is back—it is what Darius actually wants from Isla now.

Ossis / B-Ossis

Siobhan, Captain Richard, Brooke and the living-map legacy. The family’s maps remain tied to Semper Vita and the wider sea mystery.

Factions

Tempest Vanguard

The player crew and the identity built around the Tempest’s Mercy.

Agile Fleet

Fast, dominant maritime force whose history includes enslavement of sea beings and the attempted execution of Flint.

Klankus / Elias Crowe

Mechanized power structure tied to Axiom’s past and the extraction machinery beneath Wendel Wood.

Lorell Mob

Criminal/business network that controlled much of Lorell’s economy and laundered money through Machi Town businesses.

Darius Deadwater’s Watchers

Bart and Kane were sent to keep tabs on Isla; Darius describes protection, while Isla sees manipulation.

Keepers

Ancient guardian mantle tied to the Great Trees and seal. Ember is formally Level 1; Axiom is chosen but not formally completed through Session 17.
